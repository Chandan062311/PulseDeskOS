# S3 — Buyer Analysis: Monetizing an AI Agent-Orchestration Platform
Scope: open-source orchestration framework + one production support-triage app, Jev-gated supervision (100ms calibrated judgments + probability trace). Angle: BUYERS only.
Method: websearch/webfetch, Sept 2026. Every claim carries a source URL. Anything without one is marked UNSOURCED. Vendor-published research is flagged as such.

## 1. Personas: who buys agent infrastructure

### 1a. Enterprise = committee, not a single buyer (8–12 stakeholders)
- Modern enterprise AI purchase runs through **8–12 stakeholders, up from 3–5 three years ago**; budget owner is rarely the technical veto-holder. Source: https://powabase.ai/blog/enterprise-ai-decision-makers-who-really-buys-ai/ (cites Dupple 2026 buying-trends analysis)
- Typical committee spans: business/LOB owner, VP/Head of AI, CIO/CTO, 2–3 people from CISO team, legal, procurement, privacy officer (esp. EU), finance, sometimes board sponsor. Source: https://powabase.ai/blog/enterprise-ai-decision-makers-who-really-buys-ai/
- Same shape from second source: engineering (fit/perf/integration) + platform/DevOps (reliability/observability/ops) + security (data/control) + legal/compliance (regulatory/data-handling) + finance/infra (compute/GPU/cost structure). Source: https://venturemagazine.net/blog/the-hidden-buying-committee-in-ai-infrastructure-deals
- Roles that matter by function: economic buyer (CAIO/CTO/LOB SVP with P&L — costs, time-to-value in *production usage not pilot*, team confidence); plural technical buyers each with veto (solutions architect, security engineer, data engineer, ML lead — want grounding/sandboxing/failure-behavior evidence); user buyer (support ops manager / analyst lead / platform owner — usability/workflow fit); champion; blockers (usually security, legal, or rival internal build team). Source: https://powabase.ai/blog/enterprise-ai-decision-makers-who-really-buys-ai/
- Vendor-scan layer: VP Engineering / Director Data Science / Head of ML Platform runs scan, sets criteria, runs bake-off, eliminates most vendors before CTO sees shortlist; reads docs before taking calls; moved by clean API reference, concrete architecture, honest limits. Source: https://powabase.ai/blog/enterprise-ai-decision-makers-who-really-buys-ai/
- Procurement enters late with teeth: DPA, SLA, model-output indemnification, exit/data-portability, training-data-use and model-update-notification terms; having SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / DPA / SLA / SSO / audit logs / RBAC standard decides close in one quarter vs two. Source: https://powabase.ai/blog/enterprise-ai-decision-makers-who-really-buys-ai/
- LOB influence rising: Foundry data cited — line-of-business involvement in determining business needs **31%, up from 27% in 2023**; shortest path often single LOB pilot with clear ROI then expansion. Source: https://powabase.ai/blog/enterprise-ai-decision-makers-who-really-buys-ai/
- Cycle effect: 8–12 stakeholders lengthened cycles **~40–60% since 2023**; pilots die when they show demo but not retrieval quality, cost predictability, or security posture. Source: https://powabase.ai/blog/enterprise-ai-decision-makers-who-really-buys-ai/

### 1b. Startup vs enterprise behavior split
- Startup decision-maker is often technical founder or VP Engineering; evaluates product, runs POC, signs in **2–4 weeks with no RFP**; enterprise runs formal/longer process. Source: https://origami.chat/blog/enterprise-companies-building-ai-agents (vendor guide — Origami)
- Signal > size: "a 50-person startup building production agents has a bigger budget for agent infrastructure than a 5,000-person enterprise running three experimental bots"; better signals are AI-engineer count, GitHub activity, blog frequency, conference talks — not employee count. Source: https://origami.chat/blog/enterprise-companies-building-ai-agents
- Best leading indicator per same source: engineering hiring velocity (e.g., 3–5 "AI/Agent Engineer" roles in 60–90 days = active build mode, tooling need within ~90 days); engineering-blog deep-dives ≈ 6–12 months into production; Series B+ with explicit AI hiring plans = sweet spot; seed "explore AI" = 12–18 months from buying infra. Source: https://origami.chat/blog/enterprise-companies-building-ai-agents
- Titles to target: VP Engineering, Director ML Platform, Head of AI / Applied AI, Principal/Staff Engineer leading agent initiative; multi-thread all three (budget owner vs champion user vs infra gatekeeper). Source: https://origami.chat/blog/enterprise-companies-building-ai-agents
- Company-size weighting difference (VENDOR-AGGREGATED data — Presenc AI, aggregates BCG/Gartner 2026 surveys + public RFPs + own 60+ deployments; treat as directional not independent): Security/compliance SMB (<1k) **7.2** vs Enterprise (>10k) **9.6**; Integration **7.0 vs 9.1**; Observability **6.8 vs 9.0**; Capability benchmarks **8.4 vs 7.4** (SMBs care more); TCO **9.2 vs 6.4** (SMBs care far more); Vendor stability **7.2 vs 9.0**. Source: https://presenc.ai/research/enterprise-ai-agent-buying-criteria-2026
- Cycle-length split, same vendor-aggregated source: SMB **6–10 weeks**, Mid-market **3–6 months**, Enterprise Fortune 1000 **9–14 months**, Defence/regulated **12–24 months**. Source: https://presenc.ai/research/enterprise-ai-agent-buying-criteria-2026
- Discovery is bottoms-up: **41%** of enterprises primarily find AI tools via internal tech-team research vs **7%** via vendor outreach (Madrona N=150 senior decision-makers, $500M+ revenue, May 2026). Source: https://www.madrona.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Madrona-Research-Harnessing-Enterprise-Value-ROI-of-AI.pdf
- Budget ownership: most commonly centrally held rolling up to CIO, but functional leads own specific-adoption decisions; need both champion and economic buyer. Source: https://www.madrona.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Madrona-Research-Harnessing-Enterprise-Value-ROI-of-AI.pdf
- UNSOURCED GAP: startup-vs-enterprise split *specifically for orchestration-framework* buyers (vs general AI tools) — no clean survey found; above is general AI/agent-tooling evidence applied by analogy.

## 2. Deal-size / willingness signals (normalize before quoting)

### 2a. Pilot-to-program envelope
- SaaS agent pilot (Agentforce/Copilot Studio class): **$20k–$100k for 90 days** incl. platform + integration; custom-build pilot (LangGraph/Claude Agent SDK class): **$50k–$250k for 90 days** for one production-ready agent; full enterprise programme (consultancy-led, multi-workflow): **$250k–$2M+**; hybrid integration build **$30k–$250k** per major system. Source: https://thecrunch.io/agentic-ai-enterprise-buyers-guide/
- Headline platform price is often **30–50% of real bill** (API consumption, integration, prompt maintenance, escalation capacity for 5–15% needing human review, governance overhead); budget **~1.3x sticker**. Source: https://thecrunch.io/agentic-ai-enterprise-buyers-guide/
- 90-day minimum across 2–4 roles to clear novelty effects and size attach plan from measured use rate. Source: https://redresscompliance.com/enterprise-genai-pricing-report-2026

### 2b. Seat / consumption price points (list prices — enterprise pays 30–60% less)
- Median published per-seat among general-purpose enterprise AI assistants: **$25/mo** (5 publishers: Copilot $30, ChatGPT Business $20, Claude Team $25, Guru $25, Coworker $29.99); realized all-in where computable runs **1.7x–3x sticker** via prerequisites/minimums/meters; **5 of 12** tracked tiers quote-only. Source: https://coworker.ai/blog/enterprise-ai-price-index
- Enterprise discounts of **30–60% off list** are routine (AIAgentROI, public-pricing tracking only, Mar–May 2026). Source: https://aiagentroi.io/research/2026-q2-ai-agent-pricing-benchmark.html
- Microsoft Copilot structures: E7 **$99/user/mo** bundle (E5 $60 + Copilot $30 + Entra $12 + Agent 365 $15 = $117 à la carte, ~15% bundle saving) + autonomous execution metered in Copilot Credits **$200/25,000 credits/mo**; Agent 365 is governance/observability layer, build/run billed separately. Source: https://alatirok.com/enterprise-ai-agent-pricing-2026/
- Copilot Credit economics: PAYG **$0.01/credit**; monthly pack **$200/25k ($0.008, 20% off, no rollover)**; annual pre-purchase pools up to 20% off; Agent pre-purchase pool up to 15% but spans Foundry/Fabric/GitHub (portfolio lock-in risk). Source: https://licenseq.com/copilot-studio-licensing/
- Salesforce Agentforce: **$2/conversation** legacy tier, Flex Credits (~**$0.10–$0.15/action**, ~10 actions/conversation → ~$0.50–$1.50/conversation), or **$125/user/mo** seat; requires Service Cloud Enterprise+ (**from $175/user/mo**) + typically Data Cloud; implementation **$50k–$150k** + ongoing consulting **$10k–$25k/mo**. Sources: https://aiagentroi.io/research/2026-q2-ai-agent-pricing-benchmark.html and https://alatirok.com/enterprise-ai-agent-pricing-2026/ and https://fin.ai/learn/ai-customer-service-agent-pricing-comparison
- ServiceNow Now Assist: quote-only; per-"assist" meter (small task ~25 assists, large agentic ~150), field estimate **~$25–$75/user-mo equivalent**; base ITSM subscription itself **$150–$300+/fulfiller/mo** underneath. Source: https://alatirok.com/enterprise-ai-agent-pricing-2026/
- Support-triage point comps: Fin **$0.99/resolution** no platform fee; Zendesk **~$1.50/resolution** + suite $55–169/agent/mo + $50/agent/mo AI add-on; Ada **~$1.00–$3.50/interaction**, annuals from **~$30k**, enterprise **$100k–$300k+/yr**; Decagon **~$50k/yr platform + ~$0.50/resolution** custom; Gorgias **~$0.60–$1.27/resolution** by tier. Source: https://fin.ai/learn/ai-customer-service-agent-pricing-comparison
- 5,000-seat firm annual envelope: conservative role-scoped **$1.5–$2.5M**, standard broad attach **$3–$5M**, aggressive every-seat + heavy agents **$6M+**; meters: base seat **$20–$36**, AI add-on **$24–$40/user/mo**. Source: https://redresscompliance.com/enterprise-genai-pricing-report-2026
- Ceiling ≠ revenue but signals budget scale: VA–Salesforce 3-yr Agentic Enterprise License Agreement ceiling **$1.6B** (base yr + 2 options; covers Agentforce Public Sector/Health + Slack/MuleSoft/Tableau/Data 360; goal e.g. scheduling 28 days → minutes); Army–Salesforce 10-yr Missionforce ceiling **$5.6B** (Jan 2026); Agentforce ARR **$1.2B, +205% YoY** (FQ1 FY27). Source: https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/27/salesforce-won-a-16-billion-federal-ai-contract-on/
- Scale comps: Sierra outcome-priced CX platform **$150M ARR**, path to **$950M Series E at ~$15.8B** (May 2026); Google Gemini Enterprise **$21–$60+/user/mo** + tokens, **8M+ seats / ~2,800 cos**; Microsoft **230k+ orgs incl. ~90% Fortune 500**; Salesforce **$1.2B ARR**. Source: https://consulting-huber.com/ai-agent-platform-playbook.html

### 2c. Open-source framework monetization comps (closest to Pulsedesk-OS shape)
- Playbook layers: free adoption engine → self-host loss leader → managed cloud (first dollar) → enterprise skin (SSO/RBAC/audit/VPC/SLA/support — majority of revenue, **$50k–$5M ACV** cited) → network/data moat. Source: https://vikasmalpani.com/open-source-ai-business-models/
- Conversion self-host → paid: **1–4% yr 1, 5–8% mature**; single-tool cloud tier **1–3% yr 1** (50k downloads → 500–1,500 payers; at $25/mo → $12k–$37k MRR). Source: https://vikasmalpani.com/open-source-ai-business-models/ (single-author analysis — treat as heuristic, not audited benchmark)
- Reference ARR: HuggingFace **$70M ARR by 2023, 367% YoY**, **2,000+ paying enterprises** (Intel/Pfizer/Bloomberg/eBay) via Inference Endpoints (from **$0.06/hr**), Pro **$9/user/mo**, Enterprise Hub. Source: https://vikasmalpani.com/open-source-ai-business-models/
- Fine-tune/customization engagements: **$5k–$250k** by scale. Source: https://vikasmalpani.com/open-source-ai-business-models/
- Indicative commercial-license cards (small-vendor public files, NOT market rates): Startup **$4.8k/yr** / Growth **$24k/yr** / Enterprise **$96k–$100k+/yr** (Rein AI AGPL+commercial; Solvela BSL Indie $4.8k / Growth $24k / Platform custom; Sekha Startup $5k/10M calls, Business $25k/100M, Enterprise $100k/500M). Sources: https://github.com/Ai-Reign/Rein-Ai/blob/main/COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md and https://docs.solvela.ai/docs/enterprise/commercial-license and https://github.com/sekha-ai/sekha-controller/blob/main/LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md
- Seat-tax contrast example: ObjectOS AI-seat **$24/mo Team, $54/mo Business ($540/yr self-managed)**, Enterprise custom; viewers/non-AI users/devs over MCP free. Source: https://docs.objectos.ai/docs/resources/license

### 2d. Willingness direction (budgets up, but value-gated)
- **74%** plan to expand AI budgets next 12 mo (**15%** by >50%), **1%** plan cuts; **45%** carry dedicated AI line (20% each from hiring / software reallocation). Source: https://www.madrona.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Madrona-Research-Harnessing-Enterprise-Value-ROI-of-AI.pdf
- KXN (312 C/VP, 14 countries, Q4'25–Q1'26): **67%** beyond pilot (vs **31%** in 2024); median first-yr net saving **$2.4M** (**>$4M** with 3+ agents); payback ≤12 mo for **62%** with measurable ROI; avg **8.3 mo** to measurable ROI; **71%** expanding budget **>25%**. Source: https://kxntech.com/global/en/research/state-of-agentic-ai-2026/
- Pricing preference mismatch: buyers prefer **outcome-based (33% #1)** but mostly encounter **usage-based (45%)**; usage not resonating as value proxy; Verdantix (306 execs, 38 countries, 29 industries) finds buyers favour predictable, value-aligned pricing. Sources: https://www.madrona.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Madrona-Research-Harnessing-Enterprise-Value-ROI-of-AI.pdf and https://www.verdantix.com/venture/report/global-corporate-survey-2026--ai-budgets--priorities-and-tech-preferences
- Second-hand rollup (flag as reported, not primary): Gartner-via-WSJ **85%** functional leaders to increase 2026 AI spend (avg **12%** of 2025 budgets in AI); RBC **100%** allocated AI/LLM budget, **91%** dedicated; Salesforce Ventures **87%** of sellers expect to change pricing in 12–18 mo, **>50%** hybrid, **~23%** pure consumption; strongest WTP in support/service, eng agents, finance/legal, healthcare admin, claims, security, high-value sales. Source: https://startupwired.com/2026/09/07/enterprise-ai-startups-what-buyers-actually-pay-for/

## 3. Procurement objections (ranked where quantified)

> Quantified kill-share table below is VENDOR-AGGREGATED (Presenc AI). Use for ordering, not as independent truth. Corroboration from Madrona (N=150) and Prosigns noted per row.

1. **Security / compliance posture — ~32% of killed deals.** Missing SOC 2, EU AI Act gap, HIPAA-incompatible. Source: https://presenc.ai/research/enterprise-ai-agent-buying-criteria-2026
   - Corroborates: #1 purchase criterion = meeting high security/privacy/compliance bar; #2 pilot killer = lack of it. Source: https://www.madrona.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Madrona-Research-Harnessing-Enterprise-Value-ROI-of-AI.pdf
   - Floor: **80%** of mid-market SaaS RFPs now demand SOC 2 Type II (Jeeva AI July 2025, via Docket — second-hand). Source: https://www.docket.io/blog/the-2026-ai-procurement-checklist-vetting-your-ai-agent-for-security-privacy-and-trust
   - RFP asks: model hosting, training opt-outs, agent identity scoping, residency, audit trail, kill switch, certs; mapped to OWASP Agentic Top 10 / NIST AI RMF / ISO 42001 / EU AI Act / CSA AI-CAIQ. Source: https://accuroai.co/blog/enterprise-agent-rfp-30-procurement-questions
2. **Observability / tracing inadequate — ~22%.** Source: https://presenc.ai/research/enterprise-ai-agent-buying-criteria-2026
   - Corroborates: 60–72% of 2025–26 pilots stalled when prod behavior diverged from pilot without diagnosability (same vendor-aggregated source — flag). Source: https://presenc.ai/research/enterprise-ai-agent-buying-criteria-2026
   - Buyer requires per-task trace replay, tool-call accuracy by tool, failure-mode dashboards, cost-per-task, SLA dashboards; generic LLM logging fails late-stage RFPs. Source: https://presenc.ai/research/enterprise-ai-agent-buying-criteria-2026
3. **Integration gap — ~18%** (key CRM/ITSM/identity system unsupported). Source: https://presenc.ai/research/enterprise-ai-agent-buying-criteria-2026
   - Corroborates: integration complexity = **#1 pilot killer**; ease of integration = #2 purchase criterion and #3 scale-decision factor. Source: https://www.madrona.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Madrona-Research-Harnessing-Enterprise-Value-ROI-of-AI.pdf
   - Legacy integration most-cited barrier (KXN). Source: https://kxntech.com/global/en/research/state-of-agentic-ai-2026/
4. **TCO over budget — ~12%.** Source: https://presenc.ai/research/enterprise-ai-agent-buying-criteria-2026
   - Corroborates: unpredictable spend among top buyer concerns; hybrid (fixed base + usage/outcome) rising to cap exposure. Source: https://startupwired.com/2026/09/07/enterprise-ai-startups-what-buyers-actually-pay-for/
   - Mechanism: consumption meters uncapped by default (Flex/Copilot Credits/assists); need contractual caps + overage alerts + 6-mo repricing triggers. Sources: https://alatirok.com/enterprise-ai-agent-pricing-2026/ and https://aiagentroi.io/research/2026-q2-ai-agent-pricing-benchmark.html
5. **Capability gap / failed POC — ~9%** (materially smaller than ops criteria). Source: https://presenc.ai/research/enterprise-ai-agent-buying-criteria-2026
   - Corroborates: "didn't work as promised" only **6th** failure reason at **27%** (Madrona). Source: https://www.madrona.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Madrona-Research-Harnessing-Enterprise-Value-ROI-of-AI.pdf
6. **Vendor stability / lock-in — ~5% + growing.** Source kill-share: https://presenc.ai/research/enterprise-ai-agent-buying-criteria-2026
   - Corroborates: after 2 yrs buying features, **51%** rebuilt ≥1 capability in-house over lock-in/cost/quality; **67%** depend on single primary LLM vendor, only **23%** have documented contingency for failure/>50% price hike; avg **3.4x** price increase experienced in 12 mo (Prosigns 1,200 leaders). Source: https://prosigns.io/docs/prosigns-state-of-enterprise-ai-2026.pdf
   - Exit asks now standard: data/logic/audit-history portability, format/timeline/cost in contract; AELA-style unlimited deals shift leverage to renewal (negotiate caps/rate-protection/ramps upfront). Sources: https://www.kognitos.com/blog/agentic-ai-rfp-template-2026-vendor-questions/ and https://www.beri.net/article/salesforce-aela-flat-fee-agentic-pricing-2026
7. **Evals / benchmark reproducibility.** Not separately quantified in kill-share; embedded in capability + governance gates.
   - Demand: exact dataset/prompt/infra + consent to buyer-run replication; 5-scenario buyer harness (baseline, ambiguous, permission-boundary/RBAC, adversarial loop, multi-agent handoff); vendor-curated benchmarks discounted. Sources: https://traversaal.ai/blog/ai-agent-rfp-checklist-enterprise-procurement-2026 and https://www.kognitos.com/blog/agentic-ai-rfp-template-2026-vendor-questions.html (note: trailing URL as fetched; canonical https://www.kognitos.com/blog/agentic-ai-rfp-template-2026-vendor-questions/ )
   - Buyer discounts vendor ROI claims **50–70%** (vendor-aggregated claim). Source: https://presenc.ai/research/enterprise-ai-agent-buying-criteria-2026
8. **Reliability / SLA.** Contract asks: uptime SLA, MTTR, incident notification **24–72h**, model-version pinning (no silent upgrades), AIBOM on material change. Sources: https://www.docket.io/blog/the-2026-ai-procurement-checklist-vetting-your-ai-agent-for-security-privacy-and-trust and https://www.kognitos.com/blog/agentic-ai-rfp-template-2026-vendor-questions/

## 4. Where "auditability / traceability" ranks

- **Rank #3 buying criterion: production observability & tracing, 8.4/10**, up **+1.8** (fastest riser; rank 7 in 2025 → rank 3 early 2026); top 2 are security/residency/compliance **9.2** and integration depth **8.7**; capability benchmarks **5th at 7.9**, TCO 6th 7.6. Source: https://presenc.ai/research/enterprise-ai-agent-buying-criteria-2026 (VENDOR-AGGREGATED — strongest available ordering, not independent proof)
- Enterprise weight: observability **9.0** (>10k) vs **6.8** SMB; kills **~22%** of deals when inadequate. Source: https://presenc.ai/research/enterprise-ai-agent-buying-criteria-2026
- What "good" means (convergent across 4 RFP frameworks): per-run structured logs capturing intent/instruction, tools called + args (sensitive redacted), outputs, reasoning steps, model/tool/prompt versions, identities (user + agent), policy-decision rationale, response hash; NTP-synced timestamps; immutable/append-only + signed; exportable (JSON/CSV/CEF/LEEF) to SIEM; retention configurable (high-risk floor often cited **6 mo**; SOX-relevant **7 yr**, HIPAA **6 yr** examples); single-task chain retrievable in seconds; version-pinned reconstruction months later. Sources: https://www.knowlee.ai/blog/ai-act-buyers-checklist-2026 and https://traversaal.ai/blog/ai-agent-rfp-checklist-enterprise-procurement-2026 and https://accuroai.co/blog/enterprise-agent-rfp-30-procurement-questions and https://www.kognitos.com/blog/agentic-ai-rfp-template-2026-vendor-questions/
- Compliance-adjacent load-bearing evidence:
  - EU AI Act: Art 11 technical docs + Annex IV, Art 12 logging (≥6 mo high-risk), Art 13 transparency to deployers, Art 14 human oversight (mandatory approval gate, not notification), Art 86 right to explanation in clear terms; fines to **€30M or 6% global turnover** for prohibited practices; enforcement of high-risk provisions from **Aug 2, 2026**. Sources: https://www.kognitos.com/blog/agentic-ai-rfp-template-2026-vendor-questions/ and https://www.knowlee.ai/blog/ai-act-buyers-checklist-2026
  - US controls audit: COSO **Feb 23, 2026** "Internal Control Over Generative AI" requires capturing prompts/inputs/outputs/model+config versions/human-review evidence sufficient to reconstruct; PCAOB AS 2201 benchmarking provisions effective FYs beginning **Dec 15, 2026** condition reliance on AI logic not having changed. Source: https://www.kognitos.com/blog/agentic-ai-rfp-template-2026-vendor-questions/
  - Procurement artifact as compliance: 6-stage 8–10-wk track producing audit-defensible per-deployment artifact satisfying Art 9 risk-management by construction; buy wins **60–70%**, partner **15–35%**, build **3–15%** of 2026 decisions (agentmodeai vendor analysis — flag). Source: https://agentmodeai.com/enterprise-agentic-ai-procurement-playbook/
  - Governance posture in market: **78%** require HITL for Tier-2+ decisions; ISO 42001 certified **31%**, pursuing **47%**. Source: https://kxntech.com/global/en/research/state-of-agentic-ai-2026/
  - Regulated-procurement bar: provenance (SOC 2 Type II + ISO 27001/42001 or AIUC-1 with Schellman accredited Feb 2026, 51 reqs/130 controls, quarterly re-test; 3+ named refs; DPA with regions/retention/opt-out) SEPARATE from reliability (benchmarks with methodology, outcome pricing/SLA, adversarial tool-call testing, >6 mo live). Source: https://vettedaiagents.com/regulated-procurement/
  - Buyer-side governance trend: quarterly re-verification becoming contract clause; vendors with pre-built diligence packs close faster. Source: https://callsphere.ai/blog/vw9c-enterprise-ai-agent-procurement-playbook-2026 (vendor page — use for directional claim only)
- Net assessment: auditability/traceability is NOT a nice-to-have — convergent RFP evidence puts it top-3 and rising, and it is the mechanism by which security/compliance objections (the #1 killer) get discharged. Probability-trace + pinned-version replay maps directly to the Art 12 / COSO / AS 2201 reconstruction test.
- UNSOURCED GAP: no independent (non-vendor) survey isolating "traceability" WTP or rank with margin of error found; Presenc ordering is the only ranked source and is vendor-aggregated. Also UNSOURCED: that buyers will pay extra for *calibrated probabilities* per se (vs generic trace) — needs primary RFP-language count or buyer interviews.

## 5. So-what for Pulsedesk-OS monetization (buyer-lens, no new numbers)
- Sell the enterprise skin, not the framework: SSO/RBAC, VPC/residency, immutable exportable audit log, version pinning, SLA/support, DPA — this is what unblocks the 32% + 22% kill zone. Framework stays Apache-2.0 top-of-funnel; paid line is hosted + compliant distribution. Pattern source: https://vikasmalpani.com/open-source-ai-business-models/
- Price the triage app on predictable hybrid (base platform + capped outcome/consumption with 6-mo repricing trigger), because buyers prefer outcome but fear uncapped meters. Preference/mismatch sources: https://www.madrona.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Madrona-Research-Harnessing-Enterprise-Value-ROI-of-AI.pdf and https://aiagentroi.io/research/2026-q2-ai-agent-pricing-benchmark.html
- Ship procurement artifacts day one: sample immutable log schema + retention policy, kill-switch/approval-gate runbook + live demo, model/prompt-version pinning statement, SIEM export, DPA/sub-processor list, SOC 2 Type II path + ISO 42001 readiness, exit/portability clause. Checklist sources: https://traversaal.ai/blog/ai-agent-rfp-checklist-enterprise-procurement-2026 and https://accuroai.co/blog/enterprise-agent-rfp-30-procurement-questions and https://www.kognitos.com/blog/agentic-ai-rfp-template-2026-vendor-questions/
- Land via practitioner + LOB pilot (support ops), expand via platform team; discovery is bottoms-up and scale decision hinges on user love + integration cleanliness. Sources: https://www.madrona.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Madrona-Research-Harnessing-Enterprise-Value-ROI-of-AI.pdf and https://powabase.ai/blog/enterprise-ai-decision-makers-who-really-buys-ai/
